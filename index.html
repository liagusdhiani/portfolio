Brand Confidential

Leading National Bank: Unlocking Loan Disbursement Growth

Industry Fintech / Banking
Primary platforms Meta Ads · Programmatic

The Challenge

Competing in Indonesia's highly saturated unsecured consumer loan market. The client was facing massive competition where audiences endlessly compare alternatives and hesitate during long multi-field form submissions. While mass acquisition was generating high lead volumes, they suffered low approval rates, translating into high lead-acquisition costs without actual loan disbursement revenue.

Strategic Framework

  • Precision Over Volume: Shifted optimization from top-of-funnel lead forms to deep-funnel eligible borrowers.
  • Data Integration: Formed custom tracking bridges linking Meta Pixel data with the Bank's internal CRM and offline tele-sales calls.
  • Targeted Segments: Created Lookalike segments generated specifically from customers who successfully received approved disbursements, rather than generic landing page visitors.

Brand Confidential

EV Automotive Brand: Driving Brand Lift via YouTube Ads

Industry Automotive / Electric Vehicles
Platform Used YouTube Video Reach Campaigns (VRC)

The Challenge

A leading new entrant in the Indonesian Electric Vehicle market needed to drive ad recall and brand awareness. Initially, running traditional TV Commercial (TVC) broadcast formats alone on YouTube produced absolute flatlines in brand lift surveys. Audiences simply skipped or ignored the non-native asset styles, representing zero ROI on substantial impressions.

Strategic Framework

  • Creative Strategy Pivot: Shifted budget completely away from clinical corporate TVCs to native-style creator partnership assets.
  • Visual Format Optimization: Tailored ad content structures specifically matching native mobile YouTube content viewer behaviors (immediate hook within 3 seconds, natural camera angles).
